# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for helping improve Awesome Uzbek AI. You can suggest a resource by opening an issue or a pull request.

## What Belongs Here

- Resources that are useful to Uzbek speakers or to people working on the Uzbek language: apps and services that really work in Uzbek, open models, datasets, NLP tools, benchmarks, learning materials, communities and events.
- International products, when the maker documents Uzbek support or it is easy to confirm.
- Competing services. Entries are judged on usefulness, not on who maintains this list.

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Sites that present themselves as the official site of ChatGPT, OpenAI or another company without being run by that company.
- Pages made mainly to collect search traffic, link farms, affiliate or referral links, URL shorteners and links with tracking parameters.
- Dead links, pages that show nothing without signing in, and projects whose claims cannot be checked.
- Telegram bots that the maker's own website does not link to.

## Adding an Entry

1. Search the list first to avoid duplicates.
2. Add the entry to the right section, keeping the section in alphabetical order. One entry per pull request is easiest to review.
3. Use this format: `- [Name](https://example.com/) - Short description ending with a period.`
4. Describe what the resource does and who it is for, in plain English and without marketing claims. If a project reports its own benchmark results, write "the card reports ..." rather than repeating a ranking.
5. For models and datasets, end with the license from the card or repository, for example `License: Apache-2.0.` or `License not stated.`
6. For apps and services, end with a pricing label: *Free*, *Freemium*, *Paid* or *Open source*.
7. If you work on the project you are adding, say so in the pull request and add "Disclosure: maintained by ..." to the entry.
8. Write Uzbek Latin with ‘ (U+2018) in o‘ and g‘, and ’ (U+2019) for the tutuq belgisi, for example O‘zbekiston and sun’iy.
9. Run `npx awesome-lint` before opening the pull request. awesome-lint treats the Uzbek ‘ as an opening quotation mark, so "Unclosed ‘" messages on Uzbek words are expected and can be ignored.

## Updating or Removing an Entry

Open an issue if a link is broken, a service has changed, or a description is wrong. Project owners can ask for their entry to be updated or removed.
